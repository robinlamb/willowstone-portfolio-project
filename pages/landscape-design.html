Landscape Design

Beautiful landscapes begin with thoughtful design.

A great landscape is more than a collection of plants. It brings together color, texture, shade, structure, and seasonal interest to create an outdoor space that feels connected to both your home and its surroundings.

At Willowstone Landscaping, we design landscapes around the character of your property and the way you want to use it. Whether you're refreshing an established yard or beginning with a blank canvas, we'll help turn your ideas into a cohesive landscape designed to grow more beautiful with time.

Gardens & Planting Beds

From colorful flower gardens to layered foundation plantings, we create planting beds that bring life and character to your property.

We select combinations of flowers, shrubs, ornamental grasses, and other plants with attention to color, texture, mature size, and changing seasons. The result is a garden that feels carefully composed while still retaining a natural appearance.

Trees & Shrubs

Trees and shrubs provide the structure that can define a landscape for decades. They can create shade, frame views, provide privacy, soften architectural lines, and give gardens year-round interest.

We'll help select and place trees and shrubs suited to the scale and character of your property, incorporating them into the larger landscape rather than treating them as isolated additions.

Lawns & Open Spaces

A healthy lawn can provide a beautiful backdrop for gardens, trees, and outdoor living areas. We design lawn spaces with their surroundings in mind, considering how their shapes connect planting beds, walkways, patios, and other features.

For properties where a traditional lawn isn't the best choice, we can also incorporate alternative plantings and landscaped areas to create an attractive, practical outdoor space.

Designed Around You

Every landscape starts with a conversation. We'll consider your home's architecture, existing plants and features, sunlight and shade, how much maintenance you want, and how you hope to use the space.

Whether you envision a colorful garden, a quiet backyard retreat, a welcoming space for entertaining, or an entirely new landscape, our goal is to create something that feels distinctly at home on your property.

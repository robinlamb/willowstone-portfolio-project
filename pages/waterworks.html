Waterworks

Bring your landscape to life with water.

From the quiet reflection of a garden pond to the sound of a cascading waterfall, water can transform an outdoor space. Willowstone Landscaping designs and installs custom water features that complement the character of your home and landscape.

Whether you're imagining a natural-looking pond surrounded by stone and plantings, a gentle stream winding through the garden, or an elegant fountain as a focal point, we'll work with you to create a feature that feels like it has always belonged there.

Ponds & Water Gardens

A well-designed pond can become the centerpiece of an entire landscape. We create ponds in a variety of sizes and styles, incorporating natural stone, aquatic plants, and surrounding landscaping to blend each feature into its setting.

Our pond installations include circulation and filtration systems designed to keep the water moving and help maintain a healthy, attractive water feature.

Waterfalls & Streams

Add movement and sound to your landscape with a custom waterfall or stream. From a small cascade flowing into a garden pond to a series of falls built into a sloping landscape, we use stone and careful planting to create water features with a natural appearance.

Fountains

For patios, courtyards, entryways, and smaller gardens, a fountain can provide the sights and sounds of water without requiring a full pond. We can incorporate freestanding or built-in fountains into a larger landscape design.

Ready to add water to your landscape? Tell us what you have in mind, and we'll help you explore the possibilities.
